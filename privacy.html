Privacy Policy

Glidera · Last Updated: May 13, 2026 · Effective Date: May 13, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Glidera ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our mobile game. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

Key takeaway: Glidera does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to our servers. All gameplay data is stored locally on your device.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Glidera stores gameplay data exclusively on your device using Apple's standard local storage (UserDefaults). This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. It includes:

  • Game Progress: High scores, distance records, run history, total runs played, unlocked biomes, and encountered events
  • Virtual Currency: Petal balance, lifetime petals earned, and consumable item counts
  • Cosmetic Items: Unlocked and selected glider skins
  • User Preferences: Control sensitivity, haptic feedback intensity, swipe deadzone, inverted controls toggle, and reduce motion accessibility setting
  • Achievement Data: Earned badges, daily streak count, weekly tournament scores, and prestige level

This data is stored solely for gameplay functionality and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

2.2 Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, request, or have access to:

  • Names, email addresses, or contact information
  • Account credentials or passwords
  • Location data (GPS, Wi-Fi, or cellular)
  • Photos, camera, or microphone data
  • Contacts, calendars, or health data
  • Phone number or device identifiers (by us directly)
  • Browsing history or data from other apps
  • Financial or payment information

3. Third-Party Services

3.1 Google AdMob (Advertising)

Glidera displays optional rewarded video advertisements through Google AdMob. When you choose to watch an ad, Google may collect certain information as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

Information Google AdMob may collect includes:

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers (such as advertising identifiers)
  • Network Information: IP address, mobile carrier
  • Ad Interaction Data: Ads viewed, ad clicks, ad engagement metrics
  • Approximate Location: Based on IP address (not precise GPS location)

Important: We do not have access to the data Google collects. Google processes this data in accordance with their own privacy policy and applicable data protection laws.

You can limit ad personalization through your device settings:

  • Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking and disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track"
  • Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising and disable "Personalized Ads"

3.2 SKAdNetwork (Ad Attribution)

The App uses Apple's SKAdNetwork framework for privacy-preserving ad campaign measurement. SKAdNetwork provides aggregated, non-identifiable attribution data to ad networks without revealing any personal information about individual users. This is managed entirely by Apple's operating system.

3.3 No Other Third-Party Services

Glidera does not integrate:

  • Analytics services (no Firebase Analytics, Amplitude, Mixpanel, or similar)
  • Crash reporting services (no Crashlytics, Sentry, or similar)
  • Social media SDKs
  • Third-party authentication providers
  • Cloud storage or sync services

4. Local Notifications

Glidera may send local notifications to your device if you grant notification permission. These notifications are:

  • Daily Reminders: Gameplay reminders (e.g., "Your petals are waiting!")
  • Streak Reminders: Encouragement to maintain daily play streaks
  • Return Reminders: Gentle nudge after extended periods of inactivity

These notifications are generated and scheduled entirely on your device. No notification data is sent to any server. You can disable notifications at any time through Settings > Notifications > Glidera on your device.

5. In-App Purchases

Glidera uses a virtual currency system ("Petals") that is earned through gameplay and watching optional rewarded ads. All transactions within the App use this virtual currency and are processed locally on your device.

The App does not process any real-money transactions, credit card information, or payment data. No financial information is collected, stored, or transmitted.

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We Do Not Sell Your Data

We do not sell, rent, lease, or trade any user data to third parties.

Score Sharing

The App includes an optional share feature that generates a text summary of your game score. This uses Apple's native share sheet, which you control entirely. No data is shared unless you actively choose to share it.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities (e.g., a court order or government agency), though we note that we do not possess any user-identifying data to disclose.

7. Data Security

Since all gameplay data is stored locally on your device:

  • Your data is protected by your device's built-in security features (passcode, Face ID, Touch ID)
  • Data is sandboxed within the App following Apple's iOS security model
  • No data is transmitted over the internet by our code (Google AdMob manages its own secure connections)
  • Uninstalling the App permanently deletes all locally stored game data

8. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local Data: Stored on your device for as long as the App is installed. Uninstalling the App permanently deletes all game data.
  • Google AdMob Data: Retained by Google in accordance with Google's data retention policies.
  • No Server-Side Data: We do not operate servers or databases, so there is no server-side data to retain or delete.

To delete all App data, simply uninstall Glidera from your device.

9. Children's Privacy

Glidera is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Since the App does not collect any personal information from any user, there is no children's data to manage.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through a third-party service integrated in the App (such as Google AdMob), please contact Google directly through their Family Privacy Center.

Parents can manage ad personalization for their children through iOS device settings or by using Apple's Screen Time parental controls.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

For All Users

  • Delete Local Data: Uninstall the App to permanently remove all stored data
  • Control Notifications: Enable or disable notifications in device settings
  • Limit Ad Tracking: Adjust ad personalization in device privacy settings

For European Economic Area (EEA) Residents (GDPR)

  • Right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and objection
  • Since we do not collect personal data on our servers, these rights primarily apply to data held by Google AdMob. You may exercise these rights through Google's Privacy Tools

For California Residents (CCPA/CPRA)

  • Right to know, delete, and opt-out of the sale of personal information
  • We do not sell personal information
  • For data collected by Google AdMob, please refer to Google's CCPA disclosure

For Brazilian Residents (LGPD)

  • Rights under Brazil's General Data Protection Law apply to data processed by Google AdMob
  • Contact Google through their privacy tools to exercise these rights

11. International Data Transfers

Glidera itself does not transfer any data internationally. However, Google AdMob may transfer data to servers located in various countries, including the United States. Google maintains appropriate safeguards for international data transfers as described in their privacy policy.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

For significant changes, we will update the App to notify users of the revised policy.
